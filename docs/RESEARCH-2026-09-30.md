# What the best tools do that HQ did not — research, 30 September 2026

Four research passes, each against a stated baseline of what HQ already had, each
citing the vendor's own documentation. They are kept verbatim as the record of why the
waves that followed were chosen and in which order. "Baseline" in each report means the
portal as it stood before the wave; several "gaps" have since been built (see the
commit log and `docs/DESIGN.md`).

Effort scale used throughout: S ≤ 2 days, M ≤ 1–2 weeks, L more.

---

## 1. Support desk, lists and records

Method: ~35 vendor pages fetched (help centres, docs, changelogs); third-party summaries
used only where a vendor page was unreachable.

### Support — ranked gaps

1. **Collision detection / presence.** Show who is viewing/replying on the queue row and
   in the thread, and stop a reply if the thread changed underneath it. With one shared
   queue, a double reply is the most visible failure a finance customer sees. Help Scout:
   yellow corner icon = viewing, red = replying; if another reply or an unseen inbound
   arrives, "a reply … is stopped from going out" and parked as _Paused_ with Send/Edit
   (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/99-prevent-duplicate-replies-with-collision-detection).
   Front shares draft text live (https://help.front.com/t/3624vc/fronts-real-time-collision-detection).
   Data: ephemeral presence channel (user, thread, viewing|typing); thread version checked
   on send. M.
2. **Single-key actions inside the conversation.** r reply, n note, a assign, s status,
   t tag, o follow, z undo. Help Scout's full map (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/419-keyboard-shortcuts);
   Linear Triage 1/2/3/H (https://linear.app/docs/triage); Intercom surfaces them in ⌘K
   (https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6272267-how-to-use-command-k-with-intercom-inbox);
   Plain assigns the thread to whoever starts typing
   (https://www.plain.com/docs/product/platform/threads/assignment). S.
3. **@mentions in notes → notification + Mentions list.** Help Scout: notes only, "always
   show in the Notification Station" (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/694-use-mentions-in-notes);
   Front: top-5 suggestions, @all, Mentions and Subscribed sections
   (https://help.front.com/en/articles/2065). M.
4. **Followers (internal watchers), distinct from CC.** Zendesk followers are hidden, get
   public and private comments (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5179445630234-Understanding-CCs-and-followers);
   Help Scout Follow = O (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/671-follow-a-conversation). S.
5. **Time-based automations: nudge, then auto-close waiting threads.** Zendesk automations
   run hourly, must nullify their own condition, default close 96 h after Solved
   (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408832701850-About-automations-and-how-they-work);
   Help Scout "Waiting Since longer than X" → Closed, once per conversation
   (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/502-workflow-conditions-and-actions). S.
6. **Merge (and split).** Zendesk: closed ticket tagged `closed_by_merge`, permanent
   (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408882445594-Merging-tickets);
   Help Scout one at a time, "no going back" (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/31-merging-conversations);
   Pylon keeps both histories (https://support.usepylon.com/articles/6405131741-how-to-merge-issues);
   Front splits invisibly, <50 messages (https://help.front.com/en/articles/2307). Merge S, split M.
7. **Product-issue rollups with "close the loop".** Pylon: when the Linear ticket closes,
   linked issues move back to Waiting on You
   (https://support.usepylon.com/articles/2402818644-how-does-the-linear-integration-work);
   Plain's _Close the loop_ status (https://www.plain.com/docs/product/platform/threads/statuses);
   Linear Customer Requests roll count and revenue onto issues (https://linear.app/docs/customer-requests). M.
8. **SLA at-risk/breach events with a destination.** Front: "Warn when goal is at risk" →
   _SLA warning_ tag; expiry → _SLA breach_ plus notify/assign/escalate
   (https://help.front.com/en/articles/3038464); Linear notifies subscribers 24 h before
   and at breach (https://linear.app/docs/sla). S.
9. **Business hours per SLA policy.** Zendesk: per metric and priority calendar vs
   business hours, pauses in Pending (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408829459866-Defining-SLA-policies);
   schedules with holidays (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408842938522-Setting-your-schedule-with-business-hours-and-holidays). M.
10. **Ticket forms by category with required fields and attachments.** Intercom ticket
    types: per attribute visible/required
    (https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/7112127-how-to-set-up-ticket-types);
    Help Scout 10 MB/file (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/69-respond-to-conversations). M.
11. **Fewest-open auto-assignment (not round robin) + a per-person open count.** Intercom
    recommends balanced assignment for support
    (https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6553774-balanced-assignment-deep-dive);
    Front: fewest open, per-teammate limit (https://help.front.com/en/articles/2121);
    Plain filters Away/at-capacity (https://www.plain.com/docs/product/platform/threads/assignment). S.
12. **Quality metrics instead of CSAT: reopens, resolved-on-first-reply, handle time.**
    Zendesk "Tickets reopened" with Updater role = End user
    (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6456269301018); one-touch = solved with
    ≤1 agent reply (https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408820618266);
    Help Scout "Resolved on First Reply", "Handle Time" (https://docs.helpscout.com/article/380-email). S.

Also: saved-reply variables — Intercom `{{user.name|fallback:"there"}}` and macros that
also tag/assign/close (https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6433193-creating-and-managing-macros) — S.
Customer-visible status — Zendesk maps Pending → "Awaiting your reply"
(https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408825864858); a "My requests" list in the
desktop app with three statuses — M, after email.

10. **Optimistic mutations + a latency budget.** Linear's sync engine

11. **Virtualise past a threshold.** AG Grid (https://www.ag-grid.com/javascript-data-grid/dom-virtualisation/);
    Retool (https://retool.com/blog/supercharging-the-retool-table). S.
12. **Shortcut discoverability in place.** Intercom lists shortcuts inside ⌘K; Linear ?
    (https://linear.app/changelog/2021-03-25-keyboard-shortcuts-help). S.

Premium details: Help Scout stops the send and parks the draft rather than warning;
Linear's undo restores context; Plain assigns on typing; Attio's "1 of 500" header.
Wrong at this size: routing machinery, post-solve CSAT surveys, enterprise ceilings.

## 2. Customer success and SaaS revenue analytics

Scope: ~50 methodology, help-centre and practitioner pages (ChartMogul, Baremetrics,
Stripe, Paddle/ProfitWell, Maxio, SaaS Capital, SaaS Metrics Standards Board, Skok, a16z,
Gurley; Vitally, ChurnZero, Planhat, Gainsight, Custify, CSM Practice; Genroe/ziellab).

### Ranked gaps

1. **Cohort retention tables — logo and MRR, by start quarter.** Rows = firms grouped by
   first paid contract start; columns = periods since start. With ~60 firms use quarterly
   cohorts. ChartMogul: retained "as long as they remain Active"; reactivations count
   (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/159-cohort-customer-retention); MRR cell can exceed
   100% (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/160-cohort-net-mrr-retention); future intervals
   blank (https://help.chartmogul.com/hc/en-us/articles/213789989-Cohort-analysis). Stripe
   (https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/analytics). M. **Built (logo).**
2. **Seat utilisation and upsell/downsell signals.** Active seats ÷ paid seats; active =
   ≥1 activity in 30 days (https://csmpractice.com/customer-usage-metrics/); Custify
   30/60-day ratio (https://www.custify.com/blog/customer-health-score-templates/);
   ChurnZero ideal > 90% (https://info.churnzero.net/hubfs/Best%20Practices/Gettting%20Started%20With%20ChurnScores.pdf).
   Needs per-user activity. S–M.
3. **Named risk/opportunity indicators with provenance (instead of a score).** Vitally
   Indicators are rule-created, assignable, clear when the rule stops matching
   (https://docs.vitally.io/automation-playbooks-and-alerts/creating-opportunity-and-risk-alerts-with-indicators);
   Gainsight flags engagement over 90 days
   (https://support.gainsight.com/gainsight_nxt/07360/User_Guides/Build_People_Maps). M.
4. **Renewal forecast as a ledger, not a probability model.** Planhat licence fields:
   Renewal MRR, Confidence %, Weighted Amount, Notice Period
   (https://help.planhat.com/en/articles/11642904-data-model-license); Gainsight
   Renewals Due, GRR = Renewal Forecast ÷ Renewals Due
   (https://support.gainsight.com/gainsight_nxt/Renewal_Center/03User_Guides/Renewal_Center_Analytics_Calculations);

   (https://support.gainsight.com/gainsight_nxt/Renewal_Center/03User_Guides/Data_Science_Scores_in_Renewal_Center). S–M.
5. **Gross/net split: expansion rate, contraction rate, GRR beside NRR.** a16z: net churn
   "understates the losses" (https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/); 2025 median GRR 91%,
   NRR 101% (https://www.saas-capital.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/RB32WS1-2025-B2B-SaaS-Retention-Benchmarks.pdf). S. **Built.**
6. **Unified timeline.** Gainsight Timeline as the handover document
   (https://support.gainsight.com/gainsight_nxt/Timeline/01About/Timeline_Overview). M.
7. **Onboarding project templates and playbooks.** Vitally onboarding

8. **Churn-reason taxonomy, MRR lost by reason, win-back tracking.** ChartMogul
   reactivation (https://chartmogul.com/blog/reactivation-expansion-mrr/); Baremetrics

9. **Stakeholder map: champion / sponsor / decision-maker per firm.** Gainsight Sponsor

11. **ARPA trend, segment revenue over time, acquisition source.** ChartMogul ARPA

    "fewer than about 50 customers, do not run NPS" (https://ziellab.com/post/net-promoter-score-b2b-revops-guide). S.
15. **LTV — do not display yet.**

### Definitions to adopt

- Movement classification: reactivation = a formerly paying customer back on a paid plan;
  cancel-then-buy-dearer = churn + reactivation (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/163-understanding-mrr-movements).
- Churn timing: pick one and label it (https://chartmogul.com/blog/when-should-you-recognize-churn/).
- Cohort retention (logo): cohort = firms whose first paid contract started in quarter Q;
  cell(k) = firms active at the end of period k ÷ cohort size; reactivated firms count.
- LTV (when shown): ARPA ÷ six-month trailing average customer churn
  (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/208-chart-customer-lifetime-value-ltv), times gross
  margin (https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2-definitions-2/).
- Expansion MRR rate = expansion ÷ starting MRR; contraction rate likewise.
- Gross MRR churn = (churn + contraction) ÷ starting MRR (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/204-chart-gross-mrr-churn-rate).
- Net MRR churn = (churn + contraction − expansion − reactivation) ÷ starting MRR
  (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/207-chart-net-mrr-churn-rate).
- Logo churn = (churned − joined-and-churned − churned-and-reactivated) ÷ customers at
  start (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/203-chart-customer-churn-rate).
- NRR cohort-exact: MRR today from customers who were customers a year ago ÷ their MRR a
  year ago, per firm before summing (https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/grrumbling-about-retention-metrics-or-pitfalls-in-measuring-saas-churn/);
  keep the bridge-basis figure as the monthly companion (https://help.chartmogul.com/article/143-chart-net-mrr-retention).
- Seat utilisation = active seats (≥1 activity in 30 d) ÷ paid seats.

### Wrong or dishonest from contract data alone

LTV with under 12–18 months of history (https://baremetrics.com/blog/do-churn-based-ltv-calculations-mislead-us,
https://abovethecrowd.com/2012/09/04/the-dangerous-seduction-of-the-lifetime-value-ltv-formula/);
annualising monthly churn with few customers; NRR that nets across customers or admits new
ones; health scores presented as predictive without outcomes; aggregate NPS below ~50
customers; seat utilisation from account-level "last login"; overdue flags without invoice
dates; retroactive channel attribution; filled-in current-month cohort cells.

## 4. People operations and security

Sources: 28 primary (NIST 800-63B-4 2025, OWASP ASVS 5.0 + cheat sheets, HIBP API,
GitHub/Stripe/Linear/Okta/Auth0/Entra docs, Azure SQL ledger, Vanta/Drata/Secureframe,
CSA CCM v4, SIG via UpGuard, HR vendors' help centres).

Calibration: the baseline's 30 min idle / 12 h absolute sits between NIST AAL2 (idle ≤1 h,
overall ≤24 h) and AAL3 (≤15 min / ≤12 h) (https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html);
Okta's Admin Console default is 12 h / 15 min
(https://support.okta.com/help/s/article/admin-session-lifetime-idle-timeout-security-enhancements).
Drop owner/admin idle to 15 min.
